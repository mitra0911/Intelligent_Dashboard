Client presentation deck · Confidential demo narrative

The real problem is correlation

Enterprises rarely lack tools. They lack a single place to answer: What is happening right now—and what should we do first?

Teams still jump across Datadog, Splunk, APM, ITSM, ERP signals, and chat threads during an incident.

Analogy

Each tool is a radar feed. This platform is the control tower: one fused operational picture instead of disconnected screens.

What we deliver

Decision intelligence, not another chart wall

  • Unify health, incidents, noise, and tool coverage in one command view
  • Correlate metrics, logs, traces, deploys, and tickets on one timeline
  • Map technical failure to business services and dependency blast radius
  • Act with governed automation (auto vs human-in-the-loop)

From signals to decisions—with evidence and accountability.

Command Center

Situational awareness in seconds

  • Business service health with SLO burn and status
  • Active incidents with severity and likely verdict direction
  • Signal volume: raw alerts vs correlated incidents (noise story)
  • Tool coverage strip (confidence that feeds are live)

How we correlate across tools

Three anchors

  1. Time — what changed when (deploy windows, spike onset)
  2. Service — which component and which business capability
  3. Change — releases, config, flags, integrations

Analogy

Like medical triage: vitals, labs, imaging, and history are separate systems—but diagnosis needs one timeline with cited evidence.

Incident detail & RCA context

Explainable narrative

  • Correlated timeline with source badges (APM, logs, traces, deploy, ticket)
  • Domain hint and rationale—tied to visible signals, not a black box
  • Blast radius: affected service IDs and dependency context
  • Optional assistant context: structured prompt payload for guided investigation

Outcome: faster alignment in war rooms because everyone sees the same evidence chain.

Services & topology

From “what broke” to “what else is at risk”

  • Mesh-style dependency map with severity reflected on nodes and edges
  • Roll-up to business services and owning teams
  • Side context: SLIs, dependencies, recent incident linkage

Compresses questions like “who owns this?” and “is this customer-facing?” into the map itself.

Trends & reliability

From reactive firefighting to measurable improvement

  • MTTR trajectory and operational quality over time
  • Alert → incident ratio (noise vs real operational load)
  • Repeat offenders and risk/forecast-style rollups for prioritization
  • Executive-friendly scorecard view alongside engineering depth

Automation with governance

Speed without losing enterprise control

  • Auto mode — bounded, lower-risk remediations
  • Human-in-the-loop — approvals for high blast-radius actions
  • Clear audit narrative: what ran, under what policy, with what outcome

Positioning: AI-assisted operations that respects CAB, risk, and compliance boundaries.
